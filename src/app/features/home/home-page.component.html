01 /What services does Howell provide?

Howell provides Program, Design, Construction and Contract Management. Financial Management is supported through a capital financial advisor partnership.

02 /When should we bring Howell into a project?

Howell can contribute at any stage. Earlier involvement can create more opportunity to align the team, clarify priorities and connect decisions across the project lifecycle.

03 /What kinds of healthcare projects do you support?

The work spans healthcare environments such as hospitals, behavioral health facilities, operating rooms and medical simulation spaces. Project needs and scope vary by facility.

04 /Where does Howell work?

Howell focuses on healthcare in Southern California and is flexible about supporting clients elsewhere.

05 /How are owners and project teams involved?

Howell coordinates with owners, users, designers and delivery partners, helping keep responsibilities, communication and decisions connected to the owner’s priorities.

06 /Does Howell act as the general contractor?

Howell’s role is owner representation and project oversight. The specific responsibilities depend on the agreed scope; the role should not be assumed to include performing construction.

07 /How do we start a conversation?

Contact the Howell team with your facility, project stage and priorities to start discussing what support may fit.
